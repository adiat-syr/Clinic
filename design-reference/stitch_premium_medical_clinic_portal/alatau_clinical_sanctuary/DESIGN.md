## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a digital environment for a premier private medical sanctuary located against the backdrop of the Alatau mountain range in Kazakhstan. It blends rigorous Swiss-grade clinical precision with the warmth, discretion, and quiet reassurance expected of ultra-high-end private healthcare. 

The aesthetic is anchored in **Refined Clinical Modernism**—an intersection of minimalist luxury, pristine hygiene, and warm human hospitality. Interfaces must never feel sterile, alarming, or bureaucratic; instead, they evoke serenity, profound medical expertise, and effortless VIP concierge service. Whitespace is generous and intentional, borders are razor-sharp yet delicate, and interactive surfaces possess tangible physical depth through soft, light-filtering ambient elevation.

## Colors
The palette balances authoritative medical depth with refreshing biological vibrancy and soothing neutral porcelain tones.

- **Primary (`#0D6E6E`) & Deep Shade (`#0A5353`)**: Deep Medical Emerald represents restorative healing, scientific authority, and high-trust clinical excellence. Used for primary CTAs, active clinical navigation, key typography accents, and hero headers.
- **Secondary (`#14B8A6`) & Tertiary (`#2DD4BF`)**: Luminous turquoise and cyan tones inject vitality, technological precision, and diagnostic clarity. Applied to interactive highlights, step indicators, active appointment badges, and high-tech telemetry readouts.
- **Backgrounds**: Surfaces transition between pure pristine white (`#FFFFFF`) for sterile diagnostic focus and soft warm porcelain (`#F8FAFA`, `#F0F5F5`) for spacious foundational canvases.
- **Neutrals**: Typography utilizes deep midnight navy (`#0F172A`) for primary hierarchy and slate ink (`#1E293B`) for body text, avoiding harsh synthetic blacks. Supporting descriptions and metadata use muted slate (`#64748B`). Hairline dividers and crisp container borders utilize delicate mineral slate (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Semantic / Clinical Indicators**: 
  - Confirmed / Ready: Emerald (`#10B981`)
  - Pending / Awaiting Lab: Amber (`#F59E0B`)
  - Critical / Cancelled: Soft Rose (`#EF4444`)
  - In Progress / Arrived: Clinical Sky (`#0284C7`)

## Typography
The system employs a dual-typeface strategy pairing the sculpted, welcoming confidence of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and titles with the clinical precision and tabular neutrality of **Inter** for clinical data, schedules, forms, and financial metrics.

All quantitative readouts—including pricing in Kazakhstani Tenge (₸), laboratory biomarker intervals, timestamps, diagnostic codes, and calendar matrices—must strictly employ `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to guarantee vertical column alignment and cognitive clarity under clinical scrutiny.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a disciplined 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (max width `1440px`), collapsing to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

A high-ratio breathing rhythm governs clinical content: spacious canvas padding eliminates cognitive stress, while compact internal spacing inside cards ensures doctor profiles, diagnostic results, and schedule slots remain scannable without excessive scrolling. Sections maintain consistent vertical cadence (`space-2xl` for landing and clinic discovery; `space-xl` for interactive patient portals).

## Elevation & Depth
Depth mimics physical glass and high-grade surgical porcelain illuminated by warm, diffused daylight. Rather than heavy murky drop shadows, elevation relies on multi-stop ambient shadows tinted with subtle emerald undertones:

- **Surface Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Clean porcelain `#F8FAFA`.
- **Surface Level 1 (Card & Module Resting)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` resting atop canvas, bound by a crisp 1px `#E2E8F0` border, with ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(13, 110, 110, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Elevated / Hover State / Active Slots)**: Scaled card shadow: `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(13, 110, 110, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`, border shifting to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Doctor Modal / VIP Drawer / Diagnostic Viewer)**: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(10, 83, 83, 0.16), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`, paired with a frosted backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px) bg-midnight/30`).

## Shapes
Surfaces feature an organic yet architectural radius. Standard container cards utilize `rounded-2xl` (1rem / 16px) to communicate gentleness, human safety, and modern luxury. 

Interactive controls follow purposeful geometric variation:
- Buttons, input containers, and large diagnostic cards use rounded geometry (0.5rem to 1rem).
- Status tags, appointment time pills, doctor specialty chips, and notification counters utilize full pill geometry (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for fast visual categorization.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Clinical Action**: Deep medical emerald background (`#0D6E6E`), crisp white label (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded-xl`, height 48px (52px on mobile for touch accessibility). Subtle emerald hover glow (`#0A5353`), active scale `0.98`.
- **Secondary / Concierge Action**: Pure white background with 1.5px border in `#0D6E6E`, text in `#0D6E6E`. Hover fills with 4% emerald tint (`rgba(13, 110, 110, 0.04)`).
- **Ghost / Tertiary Action**: Borderless, slate text `#64748B`, hovering to `#0F172A` with soft pill background `#F1F5F9`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Specialty Badges**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `px-3.5 py-1`. Subtle porcelain teal background (`#F0FDFA`), emerald border (`#CCFBF1`), typography in `#0D6E6E` (`label-md`).
- **Status Pills**: 
  - Confirmed: Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, dot indicator `#10B981`.
  - Pending Approval: Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, dot indicator `#F59E0B`.
  - Cancelled: Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, dot indicator `#EF4444`.
  - Arrived / In Clinic: Background `#F0F9FF`, text `#075985`, dot indicator `#0284C7`.

### Time Slot Pills (Appointment Booking)
- **Available Slot**: White background, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, navy text `#1E293B`, `rounded-full`, tabular numbers (e.g., `09:30`). Hover produces soft teal border (`#14B8A6`) and subtle elevation.
- **Selected Slot**: Background `#0D6E6E`, border `#0D6E6E`, text `#FFFFFF`, accompanied by slight elevation glow.
- **Reserved / Disabled Slot**: Background `#F8FAFA`, border `#F1F5F9`, muted slate text `#94A3B8`, strike-through cursor `not-allowed`.

### Form Controls & Phone Mask
- **Text Inputs**: Height 48px, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#E2E8F0`, placeholder `#94A3B8`, text `#0F172A`. Focused state transitions border to `#0D6E6E` with an outer ring of `0 0 0 3px rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.15)`.
- **Phone Number Field**: Pre-fixed with Kazakhstan flag icon and country code (`+7`), automatically formatted with structured masks: `+7 (7XX) XXX-XX-XX` with bold tabular font alignment.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Emerald active fill (`#0D6E6E`) with white internal marker, encased in soft-rounded 4px box for checkboxes and circular border for radios.

### Cards & Profiles
- **Doctor Profile Card**: Crisp white base with delicate `#E2E8F0` border, `rounded-2xl`, structured padding `space-lg`. Features doctor portrait with clinical turquoise ring badge for years of international experience, specialty chip, star-rating with patient testimonial count, and explicit service fee formatted as `₸ XX,XXX` (`price-kzt`).
- **Diagnostic / Laboratory Metric Card**: Tabular metric readouts with baseline reference ranges, status pill indicator, and quick-download action for signed clinical PDF reports.
